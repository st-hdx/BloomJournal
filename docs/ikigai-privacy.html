Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Ikigai Finder ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Ikigai Finder does not collect or store any personally identifiable information. The answers you provide to the 9 questions are processed solely to generate your ikigai result and are not transmitted to our servers or shared with any third parties, except as described below.

AI Processing

Your answers are sent to Anthropic's Claude API via our proxy server in order to generate your personalized ikigai. This data is used only for generating your result and is not stored or used for training purposes. Please refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy for more details.

Data Storage

Your ikigai results and answers are stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
