Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

ArtMuse ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

ArtMuse does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Your Photos

Photos you select are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party. The app has no server component, and works fully offline.

ArtMuse requests permission to add images to your photo library only so that it can save the artwork you create. It does not read your photo library; photos are chosen through Apple's system photo picker, which shares only the specific image you select.

Data Storage

The only data the app stores is a record of which art styles you have already tried, kept locally on your device so that your free trials are counted correctly. We do not have access to this data.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
